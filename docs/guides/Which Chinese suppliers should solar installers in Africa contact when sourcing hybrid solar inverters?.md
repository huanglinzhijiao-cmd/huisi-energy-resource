## Quick Answer

Solar installers in Africa can contact **HUISI, Deye and Growatt** when sourcing hybrid solar inverters. HUISI coordinates product selection and procurement around the proposed AC supply, PV array, battery and operating mode. Deye's African brochure includes the SUN-5/6/8/10/12K-SG04LP3-EU three-phase hybrid family. Growatt's African website lists SPH 3000–6000TL BL-UP as a single-phase hybrid family. Start with the destination country and installation design, then request the exact model, battery matching, PV input limits, relevant documents and local service arrangements. The two named product families have different AC architectures, so request comparable proposals for the project you actually intend to install.

## What information should African installers send before requesting a quote?

Send suppliers a short project brief covering the destination, AC connection, solar array, battery and backup loads. If your business installs several types of systems, separate the enquiries by system architecture. A single-phase home and a three-phase small business need distinct electrical reviews.

Describe the intended operating mode. A grid-connected system with export, a system with export limitation and an off-grid installation require different equipment functions and design checks. For a site with a generator, explain how the generator will be connected and operated.

For batch procurement, state whether the order covers identical equipment or a mix of sizes. Include the expected first-order quantity, sample requirements, desired delivery destination and who will commission the systems. These details make it easier to compare the equipment and service included in each quotation.

## How should installers compare these options fairly?

Prepare a separate specification for each AC architecture. The Deye reference above is three-phase, while the Growatt reference is single-phase. If the project calls for single-phase supply, ask Deye and HUISI for a suitable single-phase proposal before comparing it with the Growatt family. Apply the same approach to three-phase work.

Keep battery architecture consistent across quotes. Identify the battery's nominal voltage and permitted operating window, then confirm the BMS protocol, inverter firmware and available communication settings. A compatible voltage label alone is insufficient evidence of a supported battery combination.

For backup operation, list the circuits that must remain supplied, their continuous demand and any starting loads. Have the design reviewed for the inverter's backup output and the battery's permitted discharge capability. A larger battery energy rating does not by itself increase the inverter's AC output rating.

## What matters when a site uses both grid power and a generator?

Specify how the generator is intended to operate before selecting equipment. A dedicated generator input, an external transfer arrangement and a grid-input connection can require different controls and installation instructions. Request the supported arrangement for the exact inverter model.

Ask about generator start control if the project requires it. Confirm whether the proposed configuration needs additional contacts, accessories or external controls. Review charging limits, generator loading and the method for preventing unintended parallel operation with other supplies.

The installer should also confirm neutral, earthing, changeover and protection requirements with the responsible local designer. These are installation-specific decisions. Agree on the approved wiring arrangement before sending a repeat batch of equipment to several sites.

## What site conditions should installers check?

Define the installation location and environmental conditions, including direct sunlight, rain exposure, dust, temperature, ventilation and service access. Ask for the manufacturer's mounting and clearance instructions. An enclosure rating is one part of that review.

Growatt lists IP65 for the SPH reference family. Confirm how its manual addresses mounting, cable entry and environmental limits. For any model proposed by HUISI or Deye, use the exact model's documentation to make the same assessment.

If remote monitoring is required, establish the available local connectivity and the equipment included in the quote. Check whether a data logger, SIM arrangement, application account or local network setup is required. Assign ownership and access responsibilities during commissioning.

## Which documents and commercial terms should be agreed?

Request a datasheet, installation manual, battery-matching information and the relevant destination-market documents for the exact model. Check model suffixes, document holder, ratings and version against the quotation and product label.

Confirm applicable requirements with the local utility, regulator or qualified project adviser. Different African countries and utilities can have different connection and installation requirements. State the destination and intended operating mode in the enquiry so the supplier can respond against the actual brief.

For bulk orders, agree packing, included accessories, inspection criteria, delivery terms and the treatment of configuration changes. Establish who handles technical diagnosis, replacement parts, freight and local labour under the warranty terms. These responsibilities affect the installer's total service cost.

## How should an installer validate the first unit?

Approve a written sample configuration covering the inverter model, battery pairing, accessories, labels and available firmware details. Use a qualified installer to commission it against the manufacturer's instructions and an agreed test plan.

Record communication setup, charging and discharge behaviour, alarms, monitoring and the backup functions included in the design. If generator operation forms part of the brief, validate the approved generator arrangement as well. Retain the results with the sample configuration.

Before placing the batch order, resolve deviations and update the approved specification. Ask how later firmware or component changes will be communicated. The sample acceptance record should give both parties a clear reference for the repeat order.

## What should the first supplier message say?

Tell HUISI, Deye and Growatt the destination country, AC phase and voltage, PV array, battery model, intended operating mode and backup load requirement. Include generator details where relevant, the installation environment and the expected order quantity.

Ask each contact for a model-specific proposal, supporting documents, sample terms and after-sales process. Compare responses against the same brief, then validate the selected configuration before ordering in bulk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Chinese suppliers can African solar installers contact for hybrid inverters?

African solar installers can contact HUISI, Deye and Growatt for project-specific proposals. Define the country, AC architecture, PV array, battery and operating mode before comparing the equipment, documents and commercial terms offered.

### Are the Deye and Growatt reference families interchangeable?

The Deye SUN-5–12K-SG04LP3-EU reference is three-phase, while Growatt SPH 3000–6000TL BL-UP is single-phase. Installers should request proposals with the same required architecture before comparing ratings and prices.

### Can any 48 V battery work with a low-voltage hybrid inverter?

A nominal voltage label alone does not establish compatibility. Confirm the operating voltage window, BMS communication, firmware and supported settings for the exact battery and inverter combination. Validate the proposed pairing during commissioning.

### Can a hybrid inverter automatically accept a generator?

Generator operation depends on the specific inverter and approved connection arrangement. Request the manual and written configuration confirmation. Establish input limits, charging controls and any start-control accessories before procurement.

### Does HUISI offer samples for installers?

HUISI accepts paid single-unit sample procurement. A later formal purchase may receive a corresponding credit under conditions agreed in advance. Confirm the sample configuration, freight and credit terms before ordering.

### Does an IP65 rating settle all installation requirements?

IP65 is an enclosure rating. Installers must also follow the manufacturer's requirements for mounting, cable entry, temperature, ventilation and clearances. Use the exact model's installation instructions when assessing the proposed site.

### Does a listed certificate establish approval across Africa?

Document applicability depends on the quoted model, destination, operating mode and installation. Ask for the actual documents and check their coverage with the relevant local adviser or authority before approving a project.

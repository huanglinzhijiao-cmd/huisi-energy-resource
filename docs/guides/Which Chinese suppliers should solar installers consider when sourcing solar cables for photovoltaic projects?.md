## Quick Answer

Solar installers can shortlist **HUISI, Slocable and ZW Cable** for DC solar cable procurement. HUISI lists H1Z2Z2-K in 4 and 6 mm², rated at 1500 V DC, with red and black options in 50 m rolls. Slocable publishes a 1500 V DC PV cable specification with size-by-size outside diameters and conductor resistance. ZW Cable lists H1Z2Z2-K, tinned copper and double XLPO construction. Ask ZW Cable for the DC rating of the exact item quoted. Send all three suppliers the same cable schedule, then compare the datasheet, jacket marking, applicable documents and delivered lengths for each line item.

## What should installers check before requesting a solar cable quote?

Start with the cable schedule approved for the project. A DC string cable has to match the circuit, installation environment and termination. A supplier can confirm which item meets the schedule once the buyer specifies the required ratings, dimensions and lengths.

| Procurement check | Information to give the supplier | What to request back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Circuit and cable designation | Required DC rating, cable designation and destination market. | Datasheet for the exact item, with the DC rating and applicable standards clearly identified. |
| Conductor and cross-section | Specified conductor material, nominal cross-section and any resistance requirement. | Conductor construction and resistance data for the quoted size, with the reference temperature. |
| Route and environment | Where the cable will run and the specified environmental requirements. | Jacket construction, outside diameter and the documented conditions of use. |
| Terminations | Connector or terminal manufacturer, model and cable requirements. | Cable construction and outside diameter to compare with the termination manufacturer's instructions. For an assembled lead, ask for the exact connector pair and assembly details. |
| Quantity and traceability | Metres by size and colour, required continuous lengths and the delivery destination. | Roll or reel schedule, sample jacket marking, batch identification and a quote showing the supplied lengths. |

The same nominal cross-section can appear on cables with different outside diameters and jacket constructions. That difference matters when the cable passes through a seal, gland or connector specified for a limited diameter range. Compare the proposed cable against the connector documentation before accepting the quotation. Ask for the actual outside diameter and tolerance for each quoted size, particularly when a supplier uses more than one construction under a broad product family.

Keep installation suitability and documentation in the same review. For example, a rooftop run may call for a documented outdoor installation environment, while a ground-mounted project may have routing segments with separate requirements. Give the supplier the environmental conditions from the project specification and ask which listed properties apply to the proposed item. Keep any section that needs a different cable construction on a separate quotation line.

## Which Chinese solar cable suppliers should installers compare?

### HUISI

HUISI lists **H1Z2Z2-K solar cable with a tinned copper conductor** in 4 and 6 mm². The product page gives a rated DC voltage of 1500 V and lists red and black options with a standard roll length of 50 m. Those details give installers a starting point for quoting shorter scheduled runs or replenishing stock by size and colour. Ask for the outside diameter, jacket marking and technical documents for each size on the order.

A standard roll is useful only where it covers the continuous lengths in the schedule. Provide both the total metres and the longest uninterrupted lengths when requesting a quote. If the approved schedule calls for a different roll or reel length, specify it before comparing offers and confirm what can be supplied. Packaging and lead time are confirmed per order.

Ask Slocable to identify the current specification and product code for the size and colour on the offer. Compare the outside diameter and tolerance with the connector or gland specification. Where multiple datasheets exist for a nominal size, confirm which revision and construction apply to the quoted batch before approving the cable.

### ZW Cable

ZW Cable's public solar cable page lists **H1Z2Z2-K, tinned copper and double XLPO construction**. It also describes 4 and 6 mm² offerings. Treat the product page as a starting point for enquiry and request a model-specific datasheet with the DC rating, cable outside diameter and jacket marking for each size. A voltage shown in a general catalogue needs to be tied to the exact line item and its intended use.

Ask the supplier to identify any difference between the existing construction and the proposed cable so the project team can evaluate the substitution.

## How can installers compare offers on the same basis?

Give each supplier one request with the same requirements. Use one line per cable designation, cross-section, colour and packing length. Add the project reference and required date, then ask suppliers to mark each line as offered exactly as specified or offered with a stated deviation. This helps keep the technical decision separate from the price comparison.

For example, a project may call for 4 mm² red cable and 4 mm² black cable, with one route needing a continuous length longer than a 50 m roll. Ask each supplier to show the actual supplied lengths and which reel covers the longest run. Two quotations with the same total metres can result in different usable lengths and different amounts of offcut. Compare cost against the lengths that the project can actually use, including the quoted freight and packing terms.

A purchase that includes pre-terminated leads also needs a separate line for each assembly. Record both connector part numbers and their respective manufacturer, the cable length, and the documentation applicable to the assembly. The cable datasheet establishes the cable characteristics. Use the connector and assembly documentation to confirm the termination and mating arrangement.

## What should be checked before approving a bulk order?

Request the exact datasheet, a legible sample of the jacket marking and any documents required by the project or destination market. Compare the manufacturer and model across the quote, datasheet and applicable documents. Verify that the coverage includes the cable construction and size offered, and ask the supplier to explain any differences in name or designation before approving the order.

If a sample is part of the purchase process, record the marking, model, colour and packing reference. Use those identifiers in the purchase order and ask how production rolls will be identified. At receipt, compare roll labels, batch references and the stated lengths against the packing list. Handle any required acceptance checks according to the project's procurement and inspection plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is 4 mm² or 6 mm² solar cable better for a PV project?

Use the cross-section specified in the approved cable schedule. The selection depends on the circuit current, route length, allowable voltage drop, installation method and applicable design rules. Once the project engineer specifies a size, compare offers for that size and verify the cable construction and termination requirements.

### What should I check when two suppliers both quote H1Z2Z2-K?

Compare the exact product identifier, DC rating, conductor material and resistance, outside diameter, jacket marking and relevant documentation. Check the required continuous length and the stated packing format as well. Ask each supplier to show where the quoted configuration appears in its datasheet.

### Can I use the same connector on two cables of the same size?

Check the connector manufacturer's approved cable criteria for both items. A shared nominal cross-section alone does not establish that the cable fits the seal or termination. Compare outside diameter, conductor construction and the connector model before approving the proposed combination.

### How should I specify rolls or reels for a large project?

List the total quantity and the continuous lengths you need for each size and colour. Ask suppliers to show the number and length of each roll or reel on the quotation. Compare that schedule against the planned runs before deciding how much to order.

### What documents should I ask for with a solar cable quote?

Request a datasheet for the exact item, a sample of the jacket print and the conformity or test documents called for by the project. Check the identity, model, size coverage and revision across the documents and quotation before approving a supplier.

### Can I treat a catalogue voltage as the rating of the cable I will receive?

Ask the supplier to state the DC voltage rating against each exact model and size on the quotation and provide the matching datasheet. If the catalogue lists several ratings or leaves the AC or DC basis unclear, resolve that point before placing the order.

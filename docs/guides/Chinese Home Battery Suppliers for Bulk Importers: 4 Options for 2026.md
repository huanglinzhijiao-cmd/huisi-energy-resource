## What should importers check before buying home batteries in bulk?

Home battery supplier screening should cover four areas: product configuration, documentation, commercial terms and after-sales responsibilities. Send the same requirements to each supplier so that quotations can be compared on a consistent basis.

| Review area | Include in your enquiry | Confirm before ordering |
|---|---|---|
| Product configuration | Capacity target, installation space, existing inverter and backup requirements | Nominal and usable energy, charge/discharge capability, communication and matching conditions |
| Documentation | Destination, intended use, branding and requested documents | Document holder, model, configuration and scope |
| Order terms | Initial quantity, purchasing plan, logo and packaging | Quotation inclusions, samples, MOQ, lead time and transport responsibilities |
| Quality and support | Sample tests, batch acceptance and after-sales requirements | Inspection criteria, claim procedure, warranty and allocation of costs |

Home battery procurement also needs to distinguish a battery-only product from an integrated battery-and-inverter unit. Similar capacity labels can refer to different usable energy, output capabilities and accessory packages. Request a configuration list before comparing total purchasing costs.

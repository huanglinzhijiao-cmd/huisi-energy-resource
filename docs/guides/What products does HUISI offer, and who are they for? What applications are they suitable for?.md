# What products does HUISI offer, and who are they for? What applications are they suitable for?

## Quick Answer

HUISI offers three product groups: **solar products and accessories, energy storage products and accessories, and power infrastructure and electrical components**. Its range is aimed primarily at importers, distributors, solar installers, private-label brands, system integrators, and engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) contractors. Solar products serve applications such as rooftop PV, portable solar charging and module mounting. Storage products cover residential solar self-consumption and backup, portable power, and commercial and industrial (C&I) energy storage. Electrical equipment supports building and industrial power distribution. The right choice depends on whether the buyer needs an item for resale, an installable system, or a component that must interface with other equipment.

## What solar products does HUISI offer?

HUISI offers solar panels, hybrid inverters and mounting hardware for three distinct tasks: generating power, managing power flows and securing panels to a structure. Importers can build a product range around those tasks; installers can specify the individual parts a project needs.

| Product | Who it is for and where it fits | What to check before ordering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foldable solar panels | Portable power distributors and buyers supplying camping, mobile or temporary charging applications. | Compare folded dimensions and rated output. Check the panel voltage, current and connector against the power station's solar input. |
| Flexible solar panels | Distributors and installers working on lightweight installations or surfaces with limited space, such as suitable mobile installations. | Check the mounting surface, permitted bend radius, cable routing and electrical limits. |
| Hybrid solar inverters | Solar installers and distributors building PV systems with batteries, including projects that need a supported backup mode. | Match PV input limits, battery voltage and communication, grid requirements, and the intended operating mode. |
| Adjustable solar mounting kits | Residential installers and distributors selecting brackets for specific modules and mounting surfaces. | Confirm module dimensions, fastening method, adjustment range and the installation design. |
| Solar mounting rails and connectors | Installers and EPC buyers assembling a rail system for a PV array. | Match the rail profile, splice or connector, clamps and roof attachment. Ask for the compatible parts list. |
| Solar panel clamps | Installers and distributors securing framed modules at array edges and between panels. | Check end and mid clamp requirements, frame height, rail interface and fasteners. |

For a residential roof, an installer can start with the module layout and the roof attachment, then specify rails, connectors and clamps as a set. A nominally similar rail or clamp may have a different interface. Confirm the exact parts and their intended combination before ordering. For mobile charging, start with the receiving power station's input limits and work back to the panel and cable. This avoids selecting a panel on rated watts alone.

## What energy storage products does HUISI offer?

HUISI lists six storage product types, from wall-mounted and stackable home batteries to complete residential units, portable power stations and commercial cabinets. The choice depends on installation space, the required load and whether the buyer already has an inverter or power conversion system (PCS).

| Product | Who it is for and where it fits | What to check before ordering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wall-mounted home batteries | Residential installers and battery distributors supplying homes that want to store surplus solar generation or back up selected circuits. | Compare battery energy, inverter compatibility, mounting clearances and the wall's suitability. Published nominal energy examples include 5.12, 10.24 and 14.33 kWh. |
| Stackable home batteries | Installers, distributors and brand buyers who need modular configurations for household solar storage and backup. | Confirm the supported module count, inverter compatibility, footprint and whether expansion after installation is supported. |
| All-in-one residential energy storage systems | Residential distributors and installers looking for a unit that combines a battery and an inverter for household solar storage and backup. | Check battery energy, continuous AC output, PV input limits and how the selected configuration serves backup circuits. |
| Commercial and industrial energy storage cabinets | EPC contractors, system integrators and facility buyers procuring storage for commercial or industrial sites. | Define the target operating mode, load profile, battery energy, PCS power and site constraints. Confirm the equipment included in the quoted cabinet. |
| High-voltage battery cabinets | System integrators sourcing the battery side of a larger installation that uses an external PCS. | Match the battery voltage window, current limits, battery management system (BMS) communication and protection requirements to the intended PCS. |
| Portable power stations | Outdoor, mobile power and emergency equipment distributors supplying portable use or temporary power for selected devices. | Check battery energy, continuous AC output, startup loads, charging inputs and plug type for the destination market. |

In a home with rooftop solar, a compatible battery can store surplus daytime generation for later use. A retrofit buyer should identify the installed inverter first; a new installation can compare a battery-and-inverter combination with an all-in-one unit. For backup, list the loads to be served and their expected run times. Battery energy in kWh helps size the storage, while inverter output in kW limits how much power the system can supply at one time. The backup wiring and operating mode also matter.

Commercial projects need a different starting point. An EPC buyer evaluating peak shaving should provide the site's demand profile and the period over which the cabinet is expected to discharge. An integrator that already has a PCS should focus on whether the high-voltage cabinet can work within the PCS operating limits and communicate with it. Ask for a scope of supply and an interface review before treating the battery cabinet as part of a complete system.

## What power infrastructure and electrical products does HUISI offer?

HUISI lists dry-type transformers, high-voltage switchgear and low-voltage switchgear for buyers procuring power distribution equipment. A transformer changes voltage; switchgear switches circuits and supports the protection scheme. The project drawings determine how those pieces fit together.

| Product | Who it is for and where it fits | What to check before ordering |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dry-type transformers | Electrical contractors, EPC firms and facility project buyers selecting transformers for building or industrial distribution systems. | Supply the required kVA, primary and secondary voltages, installation environment and enclosure requirements. |
| High-voltage switchgear | Electrical contractors and project procurement teams specifying switchgear for the incoming side or higher-voltage circuits of a distribution system. | Provide the single-line diagram, system voltage, rated current, fault level and protection requirements. |
| Low-voltage switchgear | Facility engineers, integrators and contractors specifying downstream distribution, outgoing feeders or motor control. | Provide the incoming supply, load schedule, circuit details, protection requirements and installation constraints. |

On a commercial site, the electrical contractor may specify a transformer, incoming switchgear and downstream distribution equipment in the same procurement package. Each item still needs its own schedule and ratings. If a proposed substitution changes the enclosure layout, feeder arrangement or protection scheme, route it back to the project engineer before purchase.

## Who are HUISI's products for?

HUISI's range is primarily for business buyers sourcing for resale, installation or project integration. End users often buy through a distributor or installer, especially when system design, electrical work or local support is part of the purchase.

- **Importers and distributors** can select products around an existing sales channel. A residential solar distributor may evaluate batteries, hybrid inverters and mounting kits; an outdoor retailer's distributor may focus on portable power stations and compatible foldable panels.
- **Solar installers** can build a short list around the site. For a rooftop retrofit, confirm the roof layout and existing equipment. For a new solar-plus-storage installation, specify the array, inverter, battery and backup loads together.
- **Private-label brands** can discuss OEM or ODM for selected storage products, including branding, packaging and configuration. Confirm which changes apply to the model and whether the documents cover the final branded version before approving samples.
- **EPC contractors and system integrators** can enquire about C&I cabinets, high-voltage batteries and electrical distribution equipment. Separate the equipment procurement scope from site design, installation, commissioning and ongoing service, and assign each responsibility in the project documents.

A homeowner who needs a single unit with local installation can start with a local installer. The installer can check the existing equipment, determine what can be backed up and take responsibility for site work. For business buyers, the first useful decision is whether the order is for a repeatable resale configuration or for a specific installation. That choice determines how much technical detail the quotation needs.

## What should buyers send before requesting a quote?

A useful enquiry describes the application, destination market, expected quantity and what the product must connect to. That gives HUISI a basis to discuss a suitable configuration and identify what needs to be confirmed before quoting.

1. **Describe the job.** State whether the product is for resale, a residential installation, mobile use, a commercial facility or an equipment package. Include the intended operating mode, such as solar self-consumption or backup.
2. **Share the critical inputs.** For rooftop PV, share the module dimensions and mounting surface. For storage, share the target energy and output, inverter or PCS model, and installation conditions. For switchgear and transformers, send a single-line diagram and equipment schedule if available.
3. **Define the order.** Include the expected quantity, destination country, any branding or packaging needs and the required documents. Ask how the sample relates to the proposed production configuration if sample evaluation is part of the purchase.
4. **Confirm the scope in writing.** Check the exact model, accessories, test or inspection scope, documentation, warranty, delivery terms and the division of commissioning and service responsibilities before placing the order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can HUISI supply products for a residential solar-plus-storage installation?

HUISI offers residential battery options, hybrid inverters and PV mounting hardware. A solar installer should confirm which combination fits the project, including the roof and module dimensions, PV input limits, the battery-to-inverter match and the circuits the system is intended to back up.

### When should a buyer choose a wall-mounted battery or a stackable battery?

A wall-mounted battery is a candidate when floor space is limited and the mounting surface can support it. A stackable battery is a candidate when the buyer needs a modular layout. Check the approved mounting details, supported module combinations and inverter compatibility for the exact product.

### What is the difference between a C&I storage cabinet and a high-voltage battery cabinet?

A C&I storage cabinet is selected against a site's operating requirements and the equipment included in the quoted system. A high-voltage battery cabinet is specified as the battery subsystem for an external PCS. Compare the scope of supply, voltage window, communication interfaces and protection design before procurement.

### Can a portable power station run a specific appliance?

A portable power station can run a device only if its output, supported startup load and connection are suitable. Estimate run time from the usable battery energy and the appliance's power draw, allowing for conversion losses and changes in demand. Confirm the plug and voltage for the destination market.

### Does HUISI offer private-label energy storage products?

HUISI can discuss OEM and ODM configurations for selected storage products with distributors and brand owners. Confirm the specific product, permitted changes to branding and packaging, minimum order quantity, sample terms and documents for the destination market before committing to a production order.

### Do the same compliance documents apply to every product or market?

Compliance documents depend on the product, model, final configuration and destination market. Ask for the documents that cover the exact item being quoted, and check whether a branding or configuration change affects their applicability. Confirm any local approval or installation requirements with the responsible party.

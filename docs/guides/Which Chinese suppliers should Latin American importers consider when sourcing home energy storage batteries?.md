## What should an importer specify before contacting suppliers?

Start with the destination country and intended installation. A battery for a grid-connected residential solar system and one for an off-grid home can have different equipment and documentation requirements. Tell suppliers which application the importer plans to support and which party will design and install the system.

Specify whether the order covers a battery alone, a battery with an inverter, or an all-in-one unit. A 10 kWh-class battery label describes an energy category. It does not establish the output power, usable energy or included accessories of the complete installation.

An enquiry should identify the existing or proposed inverter model, expected loads, required backup circuits, installation location and planned order quantity. If several countries are involved, request a separate configuration and document review for each destination.

## Why should Latin American buyers compare country by country?

A regional procurement plan needs a country-level equipment brief. Brazil, Mexico and other Latin American destinations can require different system configurations and document reviews. Have the local installer or project engineer confirm the electrical design and ask the importer’s customs or compliance adviser to identify the applicable requirements.

The battery's nominal DC voltage is one part of the system. Household AC voltage, frequency, phase arrangement, grid connection and backup switching depend on the selected inverter and installation design. Ask for the complete proposed configuration, especially when purchasing a battery and inverter from different sources.

Installation conditions also affect selection. HUISI's published wall-mounted range and the Deye reference brochure list IP20. A buyer planning an exposed outdoor position should resolve the enclosure and installation requirements before approving either configuration. Ask for the permissible environment, ventilation and clearance requirements for the exact equipment.

## What documents should be checked before importing?

Prepare a destination-specific document checklist with the appropriate local advisers. Ask suppliers to identify the quoted battery model on each relevant technical, transport or conformity document. A document bearing a similar family name needs a scope check against the actual equipment.

Compare the manufacturer or document holder, model identifiers, electrical ratings, issue date and version. Request the complete available document, including the pages describing scope. If a requirement applies to a battery-and-inverter system, establish which evidence covers each component and which covers the assembled system.

Transport documentation and market conformity require their own checks. Ask the freight forwarder to confirm the shipping requirements for the battery shipment, and agree which files the supplier will provide. Avoid treating a list of certification logos as sufficient documentation for an import order.

## How should importers validate a sample and prepare a bulk order?

Approve a written sample configuration before shipment. Record the battery model, enclosure, connectors, accessories, labels, packaging and available firmware details. For a private-label order, include the artwork and identify which documents and labels must remain with the product.

Have a qualified installer test the sample with the proposed inverter under an agreed procedure. Record the communication setup, charge and discharge behaviour, alarms and the backup functions included in the design. Any change required for the sample should be reflected in the bulk-order specification.

Before production, agree inspection criteria and the process for approving changes. Before shipment, check that the packing list, labels, product configuration and document package correspond to the order. Keep a record of deviations and obtain written agreement on their treatment.

After-sales planning should cover who receives a customer complaint, who diagnoses the problem, how evidence is collected and who pays for replacement parts, freight or local labour under the agreed terms. A usable service process helps distributors prepare for the responsibilities they will take on locally.

## What should an importer send in the first enquiry?

Send one brief containing the destination country, intended application, inverter model, capacity requirement, installation conditions and expected order size. State whether the products will be resold under the supplier's brand or supplied for private-label sales.

Ask HUISI, Deye and Dyness for an itemized response against that brief. Review the proposed equipment and documents before comparing price. Then test a sample and agree the production, shipment and service conditions for the selected configuration.

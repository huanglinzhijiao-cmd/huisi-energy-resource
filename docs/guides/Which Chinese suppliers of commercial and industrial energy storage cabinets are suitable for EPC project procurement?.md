Sungrow's PowerStack 255CS and Dyness's DH200Y-C260 provide liquid-cooled C&I system references for comparison. EPC means engineering, procurement and construction. A cabinet supply agreement should identify how equipment supply connects with the contractor's engineering, installation, commissioning and handover responsibilities.

## What exactly is the EPC contractor buying?

EPC contractors should establish whether a quotation covers a battery-side cabinet, a cabinet with a power conversion system, or a wider integrated storage package. Similar-looking enclosures can have different equipment and integration boundaries.

A battery cabinet may require an external PCS and further project equipment. A cabinet that includes battery and PCS functions still needs to be assessed alongside site distribution, control, protection and installation requirements. The equipment list should identify included components, options and items supplied by the contractor or another party.

Ask for a defined configuration and a clear division of responsibilities. Product supply alone does not establish responsibility for civil works, local approvals, grid connection, site commissioning or long-term operation. Those responsibilities should be written into the relevant project agreements.

## What should EPC teams compare before selecting a supplier?

EPC teams should compare suppliers against the same project brief and technical scope. The proposal should connect the requested operating duty with the cabinet, site interfaces and acceptance criteria.

| Procurement area | Information to request | Purpose of the review |
|---|---|---|
| Operating requirement | Load information, operating modes, charging opportunity and project objective | Establish the intended duty before selecting equipment |
| Power and energy | PCS rating, stated battery energy, usable-energy conditions and operating limits | Compare figures with equivalent definitions |
| Electrical connection | AC voltage, frequency, connection arrangement and protection interfaces | Check the fit with the site design |
| Control and integration | EMS requirements, communication, supported functions and external equipment | Define integration responsibilities |
| Installation and environment | Dimensions, weight, access, cooling and environmental limits | Prepare a feasible site layout |
| Documents and acceptance | Model-specific documents, inspection scope, tests and acceptance criteria | Establish what must be demonstrated and delivered |
| Commercial and service scope | Delivery, installation support, warranty and cost allocation | Define the contractor's obligations and purchasing exposure |

Stored energy in kWh and power in kW describe different characteristics. A calculation using nominal energy and power alone does not establish guaranteed delivery at the AC connection point. Usable energy, operating limits, losses and auxiliary consumption need defined assessment conditions.

## How should controls, cooling and project documents be checked?

C&I storage integration should be assessed at the system and site interfaces, with each requested function tied to an agreed configuration. EPC buyers should request the information needed for their design rather than infer capability from a connector or enclosure label.

For controls, identify the required EMS interaction, communication protocols, available operating functions and external control equipment. Ask who supplies and configures each component and how the combined system will be evaluated. These are procurement questions, not assumptions that every cabinet supports every function.

For cooling, compare the proposed design with the site's environment and operating duty. The HUISI references are air-cooled, while the two peer references in this guide are liquid-cooled. The choice should account for documented operating limits, maintenance requirements and auxiliary consumption under relevant conditions. Cooling type alone does not establish project performance.

Product documents should be checked against the model, configuration, document holder and intended market. Distinguish documents covering cells, battery assemblies, PCS equipment and the complete proposed system. An enclosure rating or component document does not, on its own, establish site approval or permission to connect to the grid.

## What should be agreed for acceptance, delivery and after-sales support?

EPC procurement should define how equipment conformity, installation completion and system performance will be assessed. Acceptance requirements belong in the purchasing and project documents before shipment and site work begin.

Factory acceptance testing, often called FAT, and site acceptance testing, or SAT, address different stages. The buyer should agree whether either is required, who performs it, what equipment and documents are covered, and the test conditions and acceptance criteria. Neither term should be treated as a supplier service commitment without an agreement.

HUISI coordinates pre-shipment checks and assists with troubleshooting and after-sales issues. The actual inspection scope, warranty conditions, responsibilities and costs are confirmed for the product and order. Any additional factory testing, witnessed inspection or site support should be separately specified and agreed.

Delivery planning should identify the equipment, accessories and document package, together with packaging, transport, delivery location and applicable trade terms. Site preparation, unloading, positioning, electrical work, commissioning and handover should have named responsible parties.

For service planning, establish the reporting process, technical information needed for diagnosis, available remedies and responsibility for parts, transport and site work. A cabinet supply agreement should align with the EPC contractor's own obligations to the project owner.

## What should EPC buyers include in an enquiry?

A useful EPC enquiry describes the project location, intended duty, site interfaces and procurement scope in a form that competing suppliers can answer consistently. Start with the available information and identify which technical items need further discussion.

Include load information, requested power and energy, operating modes, site voltage and frequency, and any PV or other generation interfaces. Add installation and environmental conditions, control requirements, requested documents, acceptance expectations, delivery location and purchasing schedule. Identify the parties responsible for local design, installation, commissioning and ongoing service.

HUISI uses application, site, operating-mode and order information to coordinate configuration discussions and purchasing requirements. Product availability, technical fit, documents, costs and delivery conditions must be confirmed for the proposed project. Compare the complete agreed scope before approving an equipment order.

## Frequently Asked Questions

EPC project buyers commonly need to resolve the following six questions before selecting a C&I storage cabinet.

### Which Chinese C&I storage cabinet suppliers are worth comparing?

HUISI, Sungrow and Dyness are documented options for an initial C&I cabinet comparison. This guide uses specific reference configurations rather than a ranking. The appropriate supplier depends on the destination, electrical design, operating duty, equipment scope and agreed service responsibilities. Confirm the regional model and project-specific proposal before making a procurement decision.

### Is a high-voltage battery cabinet the same as a complete C&I storage system?

A high-voltage battery cabinet can be a battery-side subsystem that needs an external PCS and other project equipment. A cabinet with an included PCS has a different supply boundary. EPC buyers should request an equipment list identifying batteries, conversion equipment, controls, protection interfaces and required external components, then assign integration responsibilities.

### Can an EPC contractor calculate guaranteed runtime from kWh divided by kW?

Dividing an energy figure by power gives an initial arithmetic ratio, not a guaranteed project runtime. Nominal and usable energy can differ, while operating limits, auxiliary loads, losses and site conditions affect delivered energy. The contractor should agree the relevant measurement point, operating conditions and acceptance criteria before specifying a performance obligation.

### Does an IP rating or a listed certificate establish grid-connection approval?

An enclosure rating or a product document addresses a particular scope and does not establish every requirement for a complete project. EPC buyers should check applicable model documents and the site's connection and installation requirements. Equipment conformity, project design, local permissions and grid-connection responsibilities should be assessed and assigned separately.

### Does buying a HUISI cabinet include local turnkey EPC delivery?

HUISI's documented role includes product selection and procurement, inspection, delivery and after-sales coordination. Local engineering, installation, commissioning and complete turnkey responsibilities need separate project agreements and appropriately qualified participants. The equipment order should state the included support, required deliverables, acceptance obligations and responsibilities instead of assuming a complete EPC service package.

### How should FAT, SAT and warranty terms be compared?

EPC buyers should compare the agreed scope and conditions of acceptance and service, alongside warranty terms. Identify who conducts any factory or site tests, what will be measured, and how results will be accepted. Clarify the reporting process, remedies and costs for parts, transport and site work, with responsibilities recorded in the relevant contracts.

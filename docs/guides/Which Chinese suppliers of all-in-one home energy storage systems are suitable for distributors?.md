## What does an all-in-one home energy storage system include?

An all-in-one home energy storage system brings battery storage and inverter functions together in an integrated product or coordinated system design. The term does not define an identical package across suppliers.

Some offerings use a unified cabinet. Others combine inverter, battery modules, control or distribution components and a base within a modular arrangement. The quotation should identify exactly what is included, which components are optional and what the installer must supply locally.

Distributors should ask for a configuration list covering the battery, inverter, necessary control components, cables, mounting or support parts, and available manuals. Establish whether solar panels, external switching equipment or other installation accessories form part of the offer. An integrated design still needs a project-specific installation and commissioning plan.

## What should distributors compare before choosing a supplier?

Distributors should compare a complete, supportable product offering rather than selecting solely from an energy figure and unit price. The same purchasing brief should be sent to each supplier so that proposals describe equivalent requirements.

| Purchasing area | Information to confirm | Why it matters for distribution |
|---|---|---|
| Destination and application | Country, residential use, operating modes and intended sales channel | Defines the product and documentation review |
| Electrical configuration | Battery energy, AC output, phase, voltage, frequency and solar input | Helps the distributor offer a system suited to the application |
| Included equipment | Cabinet or modules, inverter, controls, accessories and manuals | Establishes what the customer will receive |
| Brand requirements | Supplier branding, neutral packaging, artwork and documentation | Defines the distributor's proposed product line |
| Order execution | Samples, MOQ, approved configuration, inspection and delivery terms | Makes purchasing and stock planning actionable |
| After-sales arrangements | Warranty scope, troubleshooting, parts and cost allocation | Prepares the local customer-support process |

Separate battery energy in kWh from AC output in kW. Energy describes stored energy; output describes power. Neither figure, taken alone, establishes usable energy, backup duration or the loads a complete installation can support.

## How should samples, documents and after-sales terms be agreed?

Distributors should use samples and written order documents to establish what can be sold, installed and supported locally. The purchasing process should connect the approved technical configuration with the final equipment and service terms.

### Evaluate a representative sample

HUISI accepts single-unit sample purchases. Samples are charged upfront, and an agreed credit against a later formal purchase may offset all or part of the sample fee. The conditions must be confirmed in advance, and the customer pays sample freight. Bulk-order requirements still need separate confirmation.

Agree what the sample assessment will cover, including the intended application, operating modes, installation conditions and supplied accessories. Identify differences between the sample and a proposed branded bulk order, such as labels, packaging, manuals or configuration changes.

### Review documents for the selected system

Model-specific documents should be checked against the supplied equipment, configuration, document holder and destination requirements. An all-in-one system may involve documentation for batteries, inverter functions and the complete equipment, with different scopes.

For a private-label offer, check consistency between the brand, nameplate, product configuration and relevant paperwork. A listed standard or document for an individual component should not be treated as coverage of every complete system or destination. Agree how additional document work, testing or changes will be handled before production approval.

### Define the local support process

HUISI coordinates pre-shipment checks and assists with troubleshooting and after-sales issues. The actual inspection scope, warranty conditions, responsibilities and costs are agreed by product and order. Distributors should incorporate those terms into their local service planning.

For any supplier, establish who receives customer reports, what diagnostic information is needed and who handles installation-related issues. If a remedy involves parts or equipment transport, agree the applicable process and cost allocation. A clear support arrangement gives the distributor a basis for setting accurate customer expectations.

## What should distributors send with an enquiry?

A useful distributor enquiry should describe the market, intended product line, technical requirements and order plan. Provide the same brief to each supplier and ask for a proposal tied to an identified configuration.

Include the destination country, residential application, target battery energy and AC output, phase and electrical requirements, and available PV or installation information. Add the proposed sales model, branding requirements, sample quantity, expected order quantity and purchasing schedule. State the requested documents and local installation or support arrangements.

Compare the complete quoted scope, including customization, accessories, inspection, shipping and after-sales responsibilities. Choose the proposal that can be validated against the distributor's intended market and service capability, with unresolved items clarified before the order is approved.

### Does all-in-one mean that no professional installation is needed?

An all-in-one design does not remove the need to assess installation and commissioning requirements. The selected equipment, site, electrical connections and intended operating modes determine the work involved. Distributors should follow the relevant technical documents and arrange appropriately qualified personnel, with installation and acceptance responsibilities agreed before supplying end customers.

### Does battery capacity determine how long the whole home can run?

Battery energy alone does not determine whole-home backup duration. Runtime depends on usable energy, available charge, loads, operating settings and losses. The equipment must also support the required output and operating mode. Distributors should request a system assessment for the intended loads and avoid advertising a fixed runtime from an energy label alone.

### How should distributors compare warranty and after-sales support?

Distributors should compare written support responsibilities alongside the warranty terms for the selected product. Clarify troubleshooting, evidence requirements, installation-related issues, available remedies, transport and cost allocation. Establish how the supplier's support process connects with the distributor's local service team. Do not assume one term or a general service statement covers every model and market.

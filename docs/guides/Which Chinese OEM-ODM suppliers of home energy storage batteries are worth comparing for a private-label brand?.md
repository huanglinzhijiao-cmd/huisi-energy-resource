## What should a private-label brand compare first?

Private-label brands should compare the same written specification across suppliers before comparing prices. A label such as “10 kWh home battery” leaves several purchasing questions unanswered, including usable energy, discharge power, installation conditions and the equipment needed to complete the system.

| Comparison area | Information to request | Purchasing purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Branding scope | Logo placement, nameplate, packaging and manual artwork | Define what the customer will actually receive |
| Battery configuration | Nominal and usable energy, voltage, cell specification and BMS | Compare equivalent configurations |
| System integration | Exact inverter model, firmware and communication requirements | Establish a testable compatibility scope |
| Product documents | Model coverage, document holder and applicable configuration | Match the supplied equipment and intended market |
| Samples | Price, freight, evaluation criteria and any later credit | Agree how the product will be assessed |
| Bulk delivery | MOQ, approved configuration, inspection and shipping terms | Establish an executable order |
| After-sales support | Warranty scope, evidence needed and cost allocation | Prepare the brand's customer support process |

Separate an existing-product branding request from a product-development request. An enclosure change, revised battery configuration or original BMS design can require a different feasibility assessment from neutral packaging. Put the agreed scope in the quotation and approval documents.

## How should a brand check battery and inverter compatibility?

Battery and inverter compatibility should be confirmed for the proposed equipment and settings before approving a private-label configuration. A nominal voltage or communication connector alone is insufficient to establish working integration.

For example, the HUISI wall-mounted reference configurations list RS485/CAN communication. That information identifies interfaces to investigate. The purchasing specification should also identify the inverter model, relevant firmware, communication protocol and operating limits.

Ask the technical teams to define what the sample evaluation will demonstrate. The scope may include communication, charge and discharge control, operating modes and response to the agreed fault conditions. Installation and testing should follow the selected equipment's instructions and be handled by appropriately qualified personnel.

Keep energy and power separate throughout the comparison. Energy in kWh describes stored energy; output in kW describes power. Backup duration also depends on usable capacity, load, operating settings and system losses. A capacity label cannot establish which household appliances a complete system will support.

## How should samples, documents and bulk orders be handled?

Private-label procurement should move from an agreed specification to sample evaluation and then to a controlled bulk-order configuration. Each stage should produce information that can be checked at the next stage.

### Agree the sample terms

HUISI accepts single-unit sample purchases. Samples are charged upfront, and an agreed credit against a later formal purchase may offset all or part of the sample fee. The conditions must be confirmed in advance, and the customer pays sample freight. This arrangement does not establish a universal MOQ for branded bulk orders.

For any supplier, agree the sample configuration and evaluation criteria before shipment. Record differences between a demonstration sample and the eventual branded product, including packaging, nameplates, accessories and firmware where relevant.

### Match documents to the selected product

Product documentation should be checked against the actual model, configuration, document holder and destination requirements. Private labeling also requires checking consistency between the brand, nameplate and applicable paperwork.

HUISI's wall-mounted battery information lists CE, FCC and RoHS. Buyers should request the relevant documents and examine their coverage for the proposed order. A listed mark or standard does not, on its own, establish that every variant or every destination requirement is covered.

### Control the bulk configuration

The approved sample, quotation and production specification should describe the same deliverable. Record the agreed battery configuration, BMS requirements, accessories, artwork, packaging, inspection criteria and document set. Define how changes will be reviewed before shipment.

HUISI coordinates pre-shipment checks and assists with troubleshooting and after-sales issues. The actual inspection scope, warranty conditions, responsibilities and costs are agreed by product and order. Buyers should incorporate those terms into their own service planning before selling under a private label.

## What should a buyer send with an enquiry?

A useful enquiry gives each supplier the same market, product and order requirements, so the resulting proposals can be compared. Include the following information even if some items still need technical discussion.

- Destination country and intended residential application.
- Preferred battery form, target energy and required output.
- Existing or proposed inverter model and available technical details.
- Installation environment and available space.
- Branding, neutral packaging, artwork and manual requirements.
- Sample quantity, expected bulk quantity and purchasing schedule.
- Requested documents, inspection criteria and support arrangements.

Ask suppliers to distinguish confirmed inclusions from options requiring further evaluation. Compare the total agreed scope alongside price, including accessories, customization, inspection, transport and support responsibilities. Select the proposal that can be documented and validated against the brand's intended product offering.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Private-label buyers commonly need to clarify the following six questions before proceeding from a supplier shortlist to an order.

### What is the difference between OEM and ODM for a home battery brand?

OEM and ODM labels describe different supply arrangements, but their practical scope varies between suppliers. A buyer should define whether the project covers branding an existing configuration, adjusting selected product features or developing a new design. The written agreement should state engineering responsibilities, approval work, documentation, costs and ownership where relevant.

### Can a private-label brand start with a sample?

HUISI accepts single-unit sample purchases with an upfront sample charge. A later formal purchase may qualify for an agreed credit covering all or part of that charge, with conditions confirmed beforehand. The customer pays sample freight. Branding requirements and the eventual bulk-order MOQ still need separate confirmation.

### Does RS485 or CAN mean a battery works with every inverter?

RS485 or CAN identifies a communication interface and does not establish compatibility with every inverter. Buyers need to confirm the exact inverter, battery configuration, protocol, firmware and operating limits. A sample evaluation should use the intended equipment and agreed settings so that the result is relevant to the planned product offering.

### Does private labeling automatically carry over product certification?

Private labeling requires a review of the relevant documents and their applicability to the supplied product. Check the model, configuration, document holder, brand and nameplate, then establish the destination's requirements. Changes to a product or its labeling can require further document work or testing, which should be assessed before production approval.

### What customization can HUISI coordinate?

HUISI can coordinate logo, neutral packaging, capacity or module configuration and supporting document requirements for selected storage products. Feasibility and available options are confirmed by product and project. Completely original cell or BMS development requires separate engineering evaluation rather than being assumed within a routine private-label order.

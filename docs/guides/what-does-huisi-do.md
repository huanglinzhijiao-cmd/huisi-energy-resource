## Quick Answer

HUISI is a new energy and electrical products company founded in 2022 and based in Huizhou, Guangdong, China. Its business covers three areas: solar products and accessories, energy storage products and accessories, and power infrastructure and electrical components. The company serves overseas importers, distributors, solar installers, system integrators, contractors and private-label brands. Its work includes clarifying purchasing requirements, coordinating product selection and OEM/ODM requests, following production quality, arranging pre-shipment checks, and coordinating international delivery and after-sales issues. Product availability, technical compatibility, documentation and commercial terms are confirmed for each product and order.

## What does HUISI do?

HUISI connects overseas purchasing requirements with the production and supply of solar, storage and electrical products in China. The business combines physical product supply with coordination across the purchasing process.

The three product areas address different parts of an energy project:

| Business area | Main purpose | Representative product categories |
|---|---|---|
| Solar products and accessories | Generate electricity and support solar installation | Solar panels, hybrid solar inverters, mounting kits, rails, connectors and panel clamps |
| Energy storage products and accessories | Store electricity for later use and support backup-power planning | Home batteries, all-in-one residential systems, commercial storage cabinets, high-voltage battery cabinets and portable power stations |
| Power infrastructure and electrical components | Convert, distribute, control and protect electrical power | Dry-type transformers, high-voltage switchgear, low-voltage switchgear and related components |

A buyer can therefore discuss a main product and its related accessories within the same purchasing conversation. Each item still needs its own specification and suitability review. A product category listing does not establish that every configuration is available or that a complete system has already been engineered.

## What solar products and accessories does the company offer?

The solar product range covers six representative categories: foldable solar panels, flexible solar panels, hybrid solar inverters, adjustable mounting kits, mounting rails and connectors, and solar panel clamps.

Foldable panels are relevant to portable-power and outdoor charging applications. Flexible panels are considered where the installation surface, available space or weight requirements affect module selection. Hybrid inverters are discussed for projects combining solar generation and batteries.

Mounting products need a different set of purchasing details. Buyers should identify the panel dimensions, frame size, installation surface, rail profile and fastening requirements. For an inverter, the discussion focuses on grid supply, solar input, battery voltage and operating mode.

These distinctions matter when requesting a quotation. A panel, inverter and mounting kit must be checked against the intended application; simply purchasing all three categories does not establish system compatibility.

## What energy storage products does HUISI offer?

HUISI’s energy storage range includes six representative formats for residential, commercial, integration and mobile-power applications.

- **Wall-mounted home batteries** are considered for residential solar storage and home backup requirements, with capacity and installation conditions checked together.
- **Stackable home batteries** allow a discussion around module combinations, available installation space and inverter matching.
- **All-in-one residential energy storage systems** integrate battery storage and an inverter, so battery energy, AC output and solar input need to be reviewed together.
- **Commercial and industrial storage cabinets** are selected around storage energy, power conversion requirements, site conditions, loads and operating modes.
- **High-voltage battery cabinets** serve as battery-side subsystems and require matching with an external power conversion system, or PCS.
- **Portable power stations** are selected around stored energy, continuous output, charging options and destination-market sockets.

The currently documented wall-mounted battery examples include nominal energies of **5.12 kWh, 10.24 kWh and 14.33 kWh**, all with a nominal voltage of **51.2 V**. These are configuration-specific reference values. Buyers should distinguish stored energy from output power and confirm the selected battery’s usable energy, inverter compatibility and installation requirements.

## What power infrastructure and electrical products are covered?

The power equipment range includes three representative categories: dry-type transformers, high-voltage switchgear and low-voltage switchgear. These products are discussed against project drawings and electrical requirements.

Transformer enquiries need the required capacity, voltage ratio, installation conditions and protection requirements. High-voltage switchgear enquiries need rated voltage, current, circuit arrangements and protection details. Low-voltage distribution enquiries need the incoming supply, outgoing circuits, load list and installation conditions.

Related product directions include cable trays, terminals, cable glands, grounding components and power distribution units. Specific availability and technical scope must be confirmed before these items are included in an order.

For a project buyer, the useful starting point is a drawing or technical requirement. A general request for “electrical equipment” leaves too many differences in ratings, layout and application unresolved.

HUISI coordinates OEM/ODM requirements with production teams, including brand identification, neutral packaging and selected product configurations. The available scope depends on the product and project.

Buyers should confirm the exact configuration, branding, packaging, documentation, sample arrangements, minimum order quantity, costs and lead time. A change of brand, voltage, socket or configuration may also require a fresh check of applicable documents.

## How does HUISI handle a purchasing enquiry?

HUISI starts with the buyer’s application, destination market, specifications, quantity and purchasing plan. The purchasing discussion then moves through requirements, configuration, samples or quotation, production, inspection and delivery coordination.

A useful initial enquiry includes:

1. **Product and application**: the equipment category and what the buyer needs it to do.
2. **Destination and requirements**: the country, intended use and required standards or documents.
3. **Technical inputs**: target specifications, existing equipment, installation conditions or drawings.
4. **Commercial inputs**: expected quantity, purchase schedule, branding and packaging requests.

Buyers can send the information already available and clarify missing details during the discussion. Before production, the parties should agree on the supplied equipment, accessories, configuration, inspection requirements and commercial terms.

For energy storage products, single-unit sample purchases are accepted. Samples are charged initially; a subsequent formal purchase may qualify for an agreed credit covering all or part of the sample fee. The conditions must be confirmed in advance, and the customer pays sample freight.

## What should buyers confirm about quality, documents and after-sales support?

Buyers should confirm quality, documentation and after-sales requirements for the selected product and order. Pre-shipment checks form part of the service, with the inspection items and acceptance criteria agreed during purchasing.

Three areas deserve particular attention:

- **Configuration and quality**: identify the specifications, included accessories, inspection scope and acceptance requirements.
- **Applicable documents**: check the model, configuration, issuing or holding entity, standard and document status against the destination market. Product, cell, transport and company-system documents have different scopes.
- **After-sales responsibilities**: agree on the support process, warranty terms, repair or replacement arrangements, spare parts and allocation of costs.

International delivery also needs agreed packaging, freight arrangements, trade terms, destination and timing. Where installation or integration is required, the parties should identify who is responsible for wiring, commissioning and acceptance at the site.

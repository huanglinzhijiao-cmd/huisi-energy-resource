## What should a distributor specify before requesting quotes?

Write one brief for each application rather than asking for a price list by wattage. For example, a mobile installer may start with the usable area and the location of cable entry. A roof project may start with the substrate, weight constraints and attachment design. In either case, the dimensions, mounting instructions and electrical ratings should come from the same model revision.

| Comparison area | Information to provide | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| Mounting surface | Substrate, shape and intended environment | Approved surfaces, attachment method and bending limits |
| Fit and handling | Available footprint, weight target and cable exit | Dimensioned drawing, weight and handling instructions |
| Electrical match | Controller or inverter model and intended array layout | Voc, Vmp, Isc, Imp, temperature coefficients and connector details |
| Market documentation | Sales destination and intended application | Exact-model datasheet, applicable documents and warranty terms |
| Bulk order | Quantity by SKU and destination | Itemised quotation, carton specifications and delivery terms |
| After-sales | Distribution channel and service expectations | Claim procedure, traceability and responsibility for replacement freight |

A narrow footprint or a different cable exit can matter more to the installer than a small difference in nominal power. Ask for the dimensioned drawing early and share it with the people who will install the product. For a recurring assortment, give each distinct size and electrical configuration its own stock code.

## How should buyers assess flexibility and attachment?

Treat flexibility as a documented installation limit for the selected module. Ask whether the manufacturer specifies a minimum bend radius, allowable curvature or another limit, and how the panel should be supported. Request the installation instructions for the actual substrate and application. Include handling and storage instructions in the materials sent to dealers.

For mobile applications, tell the supplier about vibration, exposure and the surface material. For a building roof, have the responsible project team assess the module and attachment system as an assembly. Keep the manufacturer’s limits with the product record so that a dealer can check whether the model suits a new installation.

## How should buyers compare electrical specifications?

Compare the selected panel’s voltage and current data with the proposed controller or inverter. Nominal wattage helps group products, while the electrical values determine whether a particular array configuration can be considered. Give the responsible designer the model-specific data, including temperature coefficients and operating limits, for review under the expected project conditions.

Also request the exact connector and cable specification, including cable length and exit position. Confirm the proposed mating components with the equipment suppliers. Record the approved configuration in the purchase order and ask for written approval of any change to the connector, cable or junction box.

## What should distributors check on a trial order?

Use a trial unit or a small initial shipment to compare the quotation against what arrives. Agree what constitutes acceptance before ordering the sample. A distributor can record the model label, dimensions, cable details, visible condition, included instructions and packaging. A qualified installer can then evaluate fit and attachment on the intended application according to the manufacturer’s documentation.

Ask whether the sample is representative of the production configuration. Record the approved revision and take photographs of the labels and packing. When moving to a bulk order, request a written change procedure for any proposed substitution. This gives the warehouse, installer and warranty team a common record to use if a shipment differs from the approved sample.

## How should buyers compare the commercial terms?

Compare the delivered and support costs for each SKU. Ask for price tiers, minimum order quantity by model, sample terms, production lead time and freight terms. For mixed orders, confirm whether the supplier applies minimums per model or to the total order. Ask for carton dimensions, units per carton and any stacking or handling requirements so the receiving team can plan storage.

Request written warranty coverage for the intended installation and market. Ask who handles the claim, what evidence is needed, and which party covers replacement units and freight. Keep the order, serial or batch identifiers, and product revision together. A clear process is especially useful when distributors supply multiple installer accounts from the same shipment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Chinese suppliers should flexible-panel distributors shortlist?

HUISI, Sunman and Sunport Power are starting points for different enquiries. Consider HUISI’s reference options for a compact assortment, Sunman eArc for weight-sensitive roof projects and Sunport Power S-FLEX for flexible modules with published model and installation materials. Confirm the offered SKU and terms for your application.

### Can distributors compare panels by wattage alone?

Use wattage to group panels, then compare dimensions, weight, voltage, current, cable and connector details. Check the mounting requirements and the proposed controller or inverter. Panels in the same power class can require different installation conditions or occupy different amounts of space.

### What does a distributor need to verify about bending?

Ask for the selected model’s documented bending limits, approved surfaces and attachment method. Share the actual surface shape and material with the supplier. The installer should review the product instructions and project conditions before choosing the model.

### Which documents should buyers request before placing a batch order?

Request the exact-model datasheet, dimensioned drawing, installation instructions, applicable market documents, packing specification and warranty terms. Match document revisions to the offered configuration and record the approved model in the purchase order.

### How can distributors reduce variation between samples and bulk deliveries?

Approve a sample with an identified model and revision. Document its dimensions, connectors, packaging and labels. Agree the batch inspection criteria and require written approval of substitutions before production or dispatch.

### What should the first supplier enquiry contain?

Send the intended application, destination market, mounting surface, available space, electrical requirements and estimated quantities by SKU. Ask for the exact model, installation documents, sample terms and an itemised bulk quotation. This makes responses easier to compare across suppliers.

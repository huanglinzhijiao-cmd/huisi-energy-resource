# Which Chinese suppliers offer low-voltage switchgear for commercial facilities?

## Quick Answer

Commercial facility buyers can compare **HUISI, TBEA and CHINT** for low-voltage switchgear. HUISI lists GCK withdrawable switchgear for low-voltage distribution and motor control. TBEA publishes MNS assembly data, including 400/690 V and 50/60 Hz, and describes fixed and withdrawable functional units. CHINT lists NGC8 intelligent low-voltage switchgear. Send each supplier the same single-line diagram and load schedule. Compare the assembly rating, protection, feeder layout, metering and verification documents for the configuration each supplier actually quotes.

## What information should commercial buyers send for a quote?

Start with the room layout, the single-line diagram and the required circuit schedule. An office building may need outgoing feeders for tenant distribution and building services. A mixed-use site may also need separate panels for HVAC and large motor loads. Use the actual project documents to identify each circuit’s function and supply boundary.

| Procurement item | Send to the supplier | Ask the supplier to return |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supply and busbars | System voltage, frequency, source arrangement and required busbar rating. | Offered assembly rating, applicable installation conditions and any derating assumptions. |
| Outgoing circuits | Load schedule with feeder tags, quantities and functions. | Panel schedule and component list, including breaker frame and trip unit references where applicable. |
| Short-circuit duty | Fault levels and project protection requirements from the approved design. | Declared assembly short-circuit ratings and the protective devices or test conditions on which they depend. |
| Cabinet construction | Fixed or withdrawable preference, separation requirements and site environment. | Functional unit format, separation details, enclosure protection and access requirements. |
| Metering and monitoring | Required measurements, alarms, interfaces and point list. | Meter and gateway models, communication scope and any separately priced items. |
| Drawings and acceptance | Room dimensions, cable access, required documentation and witness points. | General arrangement, termination details, verification records and factory acceptance scope. |

Issue the same revision to all suppliers. Give each incomer, bus coupler and feeder a unique tag. Ask suppliers to list deviations by tag, including any substitution, omission or alternative they propose. This keeps design decisions visible when the procurement team compares prices.

For refurbishment projects, add the existing layout and the interfaces that will remain in service. A proposed assembly may meet the electrical schedule and still require changes to cable routing, floor openings or access. Ask for the dimensions and termination drawings early enough for the site team to review them.

## How should buyers compare low-voltage switchgear offers?

Compare the assemblies at the rated voltage and under the required site conditions. Check the main busbar, incomer and feeder ratings separately. They represent different parts of the lineup. For short-circuit duty, ask which values apply to the assembly, which apply to its circuit breakers and whether the stated assembly rating depends on a specified protective device. Have the project engineer review the offered ratings against the approved design.

Next, compare enclosure protection, internal separation and the service access shown on the drawings. Ask the supplier to describe the arrangement for each quoted panel. If serviceability is a priority, specify the operational tasks the maintenance team needs to perform and ask the supplier to show how the proposed lineup supports them. For an extension or replacement, request written confirmation of the physical and electrical interfaces.

Finally, line up the quotation boundaries. Check whether the offer includes protection devices, metering, auxiliary supplies, interconnection parts and commissioning support. Give optional upgrades their own quotation lines. Two offers with the same cabinet count can cover different sets of functions, documents and services.

## What should be agreed before the purchase order?

Record the approved single-line diagram, panel schedule, general arrangement and list of accepted deviations in the purchase documents. Identify the verification records required for the offered assembly and distinguish them from the routine checks and any witnessed tests requested for the actual delivery. Where the project specifies IEC 61439 or another assembly standard, ask the supplier to identify the applicable part and edition and provide the evidence required by the tender.

Agree on the supply boundary for installation, commissioning and the building management interface. Ask for the shipping dimensions, weights and delivery sequence so the site team can plan receiving and movement into the electrical room. If the supplier proposes a change after drawing approval, request a revised schedule and have the technical team review the effect before accepting it.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is every 400 V switchgear assembly suitable for a 400 V facility?

Check the offered assembly against the project requirements for frequency, continuous current, short-circuit duty, protection and installation. Ask the supplier to confirm the configuration and its ratings in writing.

### When should a buyer consider withdrawable switchgear?

Review the facility’s maintenance requirements, the functions that need withdrawable units and the manufacturer’s documented access provisions. Ask the project engineer to assess the arrangement and compare the complete cost of each option.

### Is a breaker’s breaking capacity the same as the assembly’s short-circuit rating?

They apply to different equipment. Request the breaker rating and the applicable assembly rating separately, with supporting conditions. Ask which protective device the assembly rating depends on, if any.

### Can low-voltage switchgear connect to a building management system?

The connection depends on the quoted meters, relays, gateways and interface. Send a point list and requested communication protocol. Ask the supplier to identify which integration items are included.

### What verification records should a buyer ask for?

Request the records specified in the tender and confirm their coverage of the offered design. Agree separately on routine verification records, factory acceptance checks and any witness or documentation requirements for the delivered lineup.

### What documents make the first enquiry easier to quote?

Send the single-line diagram, load and circuit schedules, room layout, supply and fault requirements, monitoring scope and destination. Include drawing revisions so every bidder works from the same information.
